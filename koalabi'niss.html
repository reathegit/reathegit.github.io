Koala Bi'niss - Animated Short Film

A 3D animated dark comedy following a koala mafia dealing with a poisoned eucalyptus crisis. Created in Blender, this project showcases character animation, cinematic storytelling, and environmental design.

As Director, I led all aspects of production including:
• Retopologizing and rigging koala models
• Scene composition and lighting
• Camera choreography
• Sound design and voice casting

The 2-minute short was a solo passion project over 6 months, blending gangster film tropes with absurdist animal humor. Featured at IndieAnim Fest 2023.
