Operation Nightfall - Final Year Project

Developed in Unity Engine, Operation Nightfall—serving as my final year project—is a hybrid RTS/dungeon crawler where you command a guerilla platoon through uncharted lands, balancing tactical overhead control with frenetic top-down combat.

As the Design Lead, I engineered the fusion of two disparate genres, planned and devised every mechanic and entity in the game, and designed every inch of the level manually, including:

• Placing every rock, shrub, tree, and collectable
• Positioning spawn points
• Planning enemy patrol routes
• Aligning weather zones
• Balancing movement speeds
• Balancing combat dynamics and damage
• Balancing lighting and colouring
• And writing all dialogue
