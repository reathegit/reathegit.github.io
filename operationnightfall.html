Operation Nightfall - Final Year Project

Developed in Unreal Engine, Operation Nightfall is a hybrid RTS/dungeon crawler where you command a squad of mercenaries through procedurally generated bunkers, balancing tactical overhead control with frenetic first-person combat.

As the Design Lead, I engineered the fusion of two disparate genres, designed the modular bunker system, and scripted AI behaviors to create emergent "helicopter-fueled chaos."

This project served as my final year thesis, exploring how contrasting gameplay loops (macro-strategy vs. micro-action) could coexist. Built with Blueprints and custom pathfinding systems.
