Bio

Growing up, I would always own pets that lived in tanks such as ants or tropical fish, as I was fascinated by designing spaces for creatures to explore and interact within. Today, as an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence for Games at Falmouth University and with a First-Class Honours in Game Design, I channel that passion into video games, designing intricate systems and spaces that spark unforgettable player moments.

I have worked with—and humbly learned from—a former Rockstar Director at Lucky Ghost, leading level design to bring immersive worlds to life. Explore my work to see how my love for world-building can inspire your next project. I’m keen to learn and excited to elevate your studio’s vision.

Career History

Design Consultant at Lucky Ghost

I was heavily involved in concept and mechanics ideation, testing and providing feedback, and was the lead level designer.

The primary design tools employed were Unity's terrain painter extension and the ProBuilder package to create both a large-scale game level and metrics level. Our team operated through GitHub, Sourcetree, Jira, and Figma.

This position allowed me the privilege of working 1-1 with a former Rockstar Director, learning directly under him and seeing how a veteran approaches team management and the design process from the bottom up.
