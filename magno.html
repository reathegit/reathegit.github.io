Magno - Puzzle-Platformer

Developed in Unity Engine, Magno is a physics-based puzzle game where you control a damaged robot with magnetic polarity powers, navigating your way through a hazardous scrap factory and out of a volcano by attracting/repelling metal objects.

As Design Lead, I designed multi-layered puzzles that escalate in complexity, conceiving of all game mechanics and handling all level designs. As our lead producer had to quit the course, I also filled their role managing asset lists, progress monitoring, and organising team meetings.

Magno was developed during my second ever game jam, its concept loosely inspired by Static Cling from Team Bugulon.
