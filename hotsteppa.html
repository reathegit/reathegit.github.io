Hot Steppa - Tangible Play

Built in Unity Engine, Hot Steppa is a reverse platformer where you play as an enraged pyro-golem who wins by destroying platforms themselves rather than reaching an endpoint. A concept inspired by Nitrome's Jack Frost, but with the added challenge of burning away the very ground you're stood on.

As Design Lead, I subverted traditional platformer mechanics by focusing on environmental destruction, conceiving of all game mechanics and handling all level designs.

As Hot Steppa was made as part of our single-screen accessibility and tangible play module, we created a custom controller using a flower arranging box with a joystick on top and two buttons on the side for your jump and pause!.
