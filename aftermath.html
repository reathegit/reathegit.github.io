Aftermath - Boss Battle Cutscene

A cinematic cutscene for a hypothetical tactical shooter created in Unity Engine, this dramatic vignette shows two soldiers discovering their fallen comrade beside a cunning enemy, losing their composure and charging in for a fight.

As Director, I led all aspects of production including:

• Designing an accompanying branching narrative structure
• Writing an accompanying fetch quest script
• Creating character cards to detail each character's personality and narrative context
• Scene composition and virtual set design
• Camera choreography
• Arranging and constructing animations
• And video editing

The cutscene demonstrates my complementary skills as a writer and narrative designer, as well as my eye for scene design and cinematography.
