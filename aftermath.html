Aftermath - Branching Narrative Cutscene

A cinematic cutscene for a hypothetical tactical shooter, created in Unreal Engine with MetaHuman characters. This emotional vignette shows two soldiers discovering their fallen comrade beside a defeated enemy, with dialogue choices affecting the scene's outcome.

As Director, I:
• Designed the branching narrative structure
• Blocked camera movements and character animations
• Implemented real-time lighting transitions
• Wrote companion lore documents and quest scripts

The project demonstrates environmental storytelling through forensic details in the scene, with a 3-minute runtime featuring two distinct endings. Created as a standalone portfolio piece to showcase cinematic design skills.
